About Asthra

St. Thomas College, Thrissur, the oldest Catholic College in Kerala, proudly presents 'Asthra 2025' - our inaugural grand mathematics fest.

This revolutionary event combines the elegance of mathematics with the vibrancy of culture and the excitement of innovation, creating an unparalleled experience for all participants.

Join us as we push boundaries, challenge perceptions, and create unforgettable moments at the intersection of logic and creativity.
